# Security Policy

This policy covers the public repositories of Virtudant AI, a nonprofit organization.

## About our forks

Many of our repositories are forks of other open-source projects.

- **The problem also exists in the original (upstream) project:** report it to that project, following its security policy. You are welcome to tell us too, so that we can pick up the fix. Please wait to share details publicly until the upstream maintainers have had a chance to respond.
- **The problem comes from a change Virtudant AI made,** or from how we build, package or ship the code: report it to us.
- **You are not sure:** report it to us.

A security file inside a fork may belong to the upstream project. Its contacts, rewards and promises are theirs, not ours.

## Supported versions

For our own changes, we fix security problems on the default branch and in our latest release, if there is one. We do not patch older versions.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not report security problems in public issues, pull requests or discussions.**

Email **cajdiaz.dev@gmail.com**.

Please include:

- the repository, and the branch, version or commit;
- what you found and what an attacker could do with it;
- steps to reproduce it, with a proof of concept if you have one;
- your operating system or device, if it matters;
- whether you have reported it anywhere else.

Do not include other people's personal data or working credentials.

## What to expect

- We confirm that we received your report within 14 days.
- We investigate and tell you whether we can reproduce it and what we plan to do.
- We tell you when a fix is released.
- If the problem is upstream, we point you to the right project.
- We credit you when we publish the fix, unless you prefer not to be named.

We ask that you give us a reasonable time to fix the problem before you disclose it, and that you agree on a disclosure date with us. We suggest 90 days from your report.

We do not offer a paid bug bounty.

## Good-faith research

We will not take legal action against you for security research that follows this policy and is done in good faith. This means that you:

- test only your own copies, accounts and devices;
- do not access, change or delete data that is not yours, and if you come across such data, you stop, tell us, and delete any copy;
- do not harm users or disrupt services;
- do not disclose the problem before the agreed date.

This applies only to claims Virtudant AI could bring. It cannot authorize testing of systems that belong to others, including upstream projects, GitHub and other service providers.

## Out of scope

- Problems only in upstream or third-party code that we have not changed (report them to their maintainers)
- Social engineering or phishing of our people
- Denial-of-service testing
- Automated scanner output with no demonstrated impact
